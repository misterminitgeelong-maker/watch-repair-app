# Google Play listing text (draft)

**App name (max 30):** Mainspring

**Short description (max 80):**
Run your watch, shoe and key repair shop: jobs, quotes, invoices and photos.

**Full description (max 4000):**
Mainspring is the repair business platform for watch, shoe and key repairers. Keep every job, customer, quote and invoice in one place, and run your bench from your phone.

What you can do
- Take in jobs and track them from intake to collection
- Photograph items with your phone camera and attach them to the job
- Send quotes and get customer approval online
- Raise invoices and record payments
- Keep customer details and job history together
- Get a notification when a customer approves a quote, pays an invoice or sends a message
- See how the shop is performing on the dashboard

Mainspring works for a single shop or a network of shops. You need a Mainspring shop account to sign in. Create one at mainspring.au.
